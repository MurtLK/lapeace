Example to review with a fluent Punjabi speaker: pa_in_1555

Reference: ਚਰਚ ਦੇ ਪਰਿਸਰ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੁਝ ਕਬਰਾਂ ਉੱਤੇ ਕੁਝ ਕਬੂਤਰਾਂ ਦੀਆਂ ਦਿਲਚਸਪ ਸੰਗਮਰਮਰ ਦੀਆਂ ਮੂਰਤੀਆਂ ਹਨ।

Scribe (language set to Punjabi): Църквите в перисъл изкуч кавра, у те куч кавутери биле девчас ки самарна диня мортиня.

Scribe wrote the Punjabi sounds in Cyrillic letters but still reported "Punjabi, probability 1". A system that trusts its own language label wouldn't notice. Much of the other error is spelling rather than mishearing: Hindi and Urdu outputs write English loanwords like "call" in Latin script.
