# Presentation assets

Three 1920 × 1080 PNGs are ready to insert into a slide. Do not crop away the sample sizes, model labels or interpretation notes without retaining them elsewhere on that slide.

- `01-accent-word-error.png`: accented-English WER by first-language cohort; original Buzz model unknown.
- `02-paired-routing.png`: supplied-text versus local Whisper Tiny routing on the same 40 banking requests.
- `03-ward-context.png`: exploratory ward index alongside covered population and coverage.

The three aggregate CSVs contain full-precision numeric values for editable charts. `chart-data.json` also records the exact source-run IDs and source artifact hashes. These small aggregate exports are presentation material; they do not replace the original raw evidence or its limitations.
